- The arrow no longer leads to a quest's hand-in while the quest still has an
  open objective in your log. It leads to that objective first, also when you
  skipped its step, when the guide lists it as optional, when it comes after
  the hand-in, or when the guide has no step for it. The arrow leads to the
  hand-in once the game counts the quest ready.
