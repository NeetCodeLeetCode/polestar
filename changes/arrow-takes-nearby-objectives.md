- New setting "Take nearby objectives" (Guide, Window, Dynamic
  Questing), off by default: the arrow leads to a suggested objective by
  itself, as if you had clicked it, when the walk is worth it. It weighs the
  XP of the quest against the extra walking, counts a quest that opens a
  follow-up or frees a log slot for more, and picks the best order when
  several objectives are near. It goes back to the guide when the objective
  is done, you walk away or you dismiss it. "Objectives: how far out of your
  way" sets the most extra seconds of walking.
