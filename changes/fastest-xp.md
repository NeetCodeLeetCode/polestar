- New setting "Fastest XP" (Guide, Automation, first in Quick settings, `/polestar fast`),
  off by default: one switch that sets the leveling settings to their fastest values
  (Dynamic Questing, Take nearby objectives, Reorder the next steps, Skip grey and slow
  quests, rare-drop quests hidden, deathskips, travel planning and auto accept and turn-in).
  Chat lists what it changed. A setting you change by hand afterwards stays yours, and the
  row says "customized". Off puts back what it replaced. Solo or group, dungeons and
  gathering stay your choice. A Hardcore character keeps Take nearby objectives and
  Use deathskips as they are.
