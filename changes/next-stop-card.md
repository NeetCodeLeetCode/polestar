- With Fastest XP on in a level-range guide, a card now names the best next hub or zone for XP
  for each minute when the guide's arrow is five minutes away or more. Go starts the trip;
  the arrow never moves by itself. New setting "Suggest the next stop" (Guide, Automation,
  Fastest XP; on by default, and only used while Fastest XP is on).
- The card can also say "Go do (dungeon) now": a dungeon in range with quests ready to take,
  only for a group of three or more or when "Dungeons in my route" is not "Skip them".
