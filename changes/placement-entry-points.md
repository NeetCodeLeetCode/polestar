- A guide tab you come back to, and the open guide when you reach a new zone,
  are now placed again from your quest log if you played on since they were
  last placed. Before, only a login, a reload, a new tab or Reset guide
  progress placed a guide, so a tab you left open while you played in another
  guide stayed at its old step. Your own ticks and skips stay as they are, and
  a placement you undid stays undone. A placement that marks something still
  shows its note with an Undo.
