- New setting "Route advice" (Guide, Automation, Fastest XP), off by default, and on with
  Fastest XP. A card tells you when a quest in your log is far off your route ("We recommend
  abandoning it"), when you did not pick up a quest on it ("We recommend taking it" or
  "leaving it"), and when a quest players skip is in your log. It judges route distance only,
  never kill counts or drop chances. It never abandons by itself: Abandon asks the game first,
  and Keep it stops the card for that quest. One card at a time, at most two an hour.
