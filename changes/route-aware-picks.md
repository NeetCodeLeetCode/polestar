- A vendor, an inn or a trainer that your guide leaves to the nearest one is
  now the one on your way. Before, the arrow led to the nearest one even when
  it sat off the route, such as a vendor north of you while the guide went
  south. A nearer one still wins when it costs no more than 15 seconds of
  detour, and a guide with nothing ahead of the step keeps the nearest one.
