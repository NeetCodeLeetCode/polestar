- The routing report (Copy routing report, `/polestar routing`) now says why the
  arrow points where it does. It names who owns the arrow (the guide, a trip such as
  Next stop, your own waypoint, an objective the arrow took), how far the guide's own
  goal is, and the last times the arrow changed, with the event behind each change.
  It lists each place Next stop weighed with its numbers and why it won or lost, the
  XP rate and the guide's pace, Fastest XP and the route advice it judged. Each
  nearby objective also says why it lost. The report holds no character, realm,
  guild, account or party names.
