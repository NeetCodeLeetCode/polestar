- The guide menu has a new row, Copy routing report, and `/polestar routing`
  does the same. It opens a report of why the arrow and the next steps were
  chosen: the step order, the nearby objectives and how each scored, and your
  routing settings. The report is selected, so Ctrl+C copies it. It holds no
  character, realm, guild, account or party names.
