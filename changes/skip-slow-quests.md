- New setting "Skip slow quests" (Guide, Automation, Quests), off by default:
  leaves out a short list of quests that cost far more time than they pay (a
  rare drop, slow respawns, little XP for the time). A quest you took stays,
  so does one a later quest needs, and class quests are never left out. The
  guide window says what it left out.
