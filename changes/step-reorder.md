- New setting "Reorder the next steps" (Guide, Window, Dynamic Questing),
  off by default. When the next few steps of the guide can be done in an
  order that walks less, the window shows the step to do first. The guide,
  its steps and your progress stay as they are, and the guide's own step
  comes back when the other one is done. A step keeps its place when it
  shares a quest with an earlier step, needs a quest you do not have yet, or
  sits next to a flight, a dungeon or a timed quest. "Recalibrate the arrow"
  chooses the order again.
  The order also counts the walk on to a class trainer that comes after the
  steps it looks at.
