- New "Sync with my quests" in the guide window's menu, also `/polestar sync`.
  If your guide is behind your quests, it places the guide again from your
  quest log: it marks the steps your quests show you passed and moves you to
  the work you have started. Your own check-offs and skips stay. The window
  says what it marked, with an Undo.
